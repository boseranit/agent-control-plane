The research experiment controller is designed to mimic a real quantitative research workflow done by experienced researchers who are
coming up with testable hypotheses and verifying them rigorously, and who also have a dedicated programmer for implementing their ideas.
Moreover this workflow occurs in a loop so that every time we run an experiment the next experiment is supposed build on top of the
insights gained from before, but may also come up with a completely different idea if there's something more promising. Currently a
research run will have an agenda specified by a human and the idea is that based on that initial research spec, in each iteration we should
have different variations of that idea, or related to/loosely motivated by that spec, hypothesized and tested. Follow-ups that have
been recommended based on the evaluation of previous runs should be accounted for.

The aim is to maximise learning quality without adding extra orchestration phases or artifact sprawl.
